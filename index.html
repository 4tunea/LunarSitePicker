      How sites are ranked

      A site is recommended when the Sun is up and Earth is in view on the landing date, and both stay that way for at least 5 days. The score is 60% time with both after landing (up to 14 days), 20% share of the lunar day in sunlight, 20% share with Earth in view. Terrain, slopes and hazards are not included.

      Technical data for this site

      Positions come from an approximate model that runs in this page (truncated Meeus lunar theory with optical librations, about 0.1° accuracy). The horizon is a smooth sphere, so terrain is not included. Near the poles the Sun stays within about 1.5° of the horizon, so real ridges and crater rims change these results a lot. The Sun counts as up when any part of its disc is above the horizon, Earth when its centre is.
