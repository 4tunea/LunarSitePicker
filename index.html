      Technical data for this site

      Sun and Earth positions come from a model that runs in this tool (truncated Meeus lunar theory with optical librations, about 0.1° accuracy). Where terrain data is loaded, the skyline comes from NASA's LOLA elevation model, seen from 2 m above the ground; it shows mountains and crater rims, not boulders. The Sun counts as up when any part of its disc clears the skyline, Earth when its centre does.
